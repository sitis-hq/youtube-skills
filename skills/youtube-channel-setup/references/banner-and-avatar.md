# Banner and profile picture

Design rules rewritten from sitis-skills `youtube-channel-banner-generate` and `user-avatar-generate`
(sitis-hq/sitis-skills, commit dab9dd9). Dropped from them: the unsourced percentages ("+40 %
subscriptions", "blue +34 % trust", PhotoFeeler deltas), gender and ethnicity "optimisation" of
faces, Midjourney flags (`--ar`, `--style raw`) and outpainting parameters. Images are generated by
the `poyo` tool attached to the step; its own help says which models and sizes it offers.

## Specification

YouTube Help "Manage your channel branding" (support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525), read
2026-11-04:

| | Banner | Profile picture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Size | 2560×1440 recommended; 2048×1152 minimum; 16:9 | renders at 98×98; upload 800×800 (the long-standing recommendation; not on the page now) |
| Safe area | 1235×338 at 2048×1152 = 1546×423 at 2560×1440, centred | the inscribed circle |
| File | 6 MB or smaller | JPG, GIF, BMP or PNG, no animated GIF, 15 MB or smaller |

Device areas of a 2560×1440 banner, all centred (YouTube's channel-art template; the current help
page gives only the safe area): TV 2560×1440, desktop 2560×423, tablet 1855×423, mobile 1546×423.
Older notes that say "safe area 1235×338" give the minimum-size figure; on a 2560 canvas it is
1546×423.

## Banner

What it must do: tell a visitor in a glance whose channel this is and what they get.

- **Everything that must be read sits inside 1546×423**, with a margin — not touching its edges:
  channel name or logo, one line of promise (what the channel is), optionally the release rhythm
  ("new music every Friday"). At most two text elements.
- **Design the safe area first**, then extend: the desktop and tablet strips carry continuation of
  the background, the rest of the 1440 height is seen only on TV and carries atmosphere, nothing
  essential.
- **Text size**: the safe area shrinks to a phone's width (about 390 px CSS), roughly a quarter of
  its pixels. Check `banner-phone.png`: if the promise line is not readable there, it is too small.
  Heavy sans-serif, strong contrast with what is behind it (aim for WCAG 4.5:1 for text), no thin or
  script faces.
- **One message, asymmetric composition**: a subject on the left or right third of the safe area,
  text in the clear space beside it. No collage.
- **Same palette and type as the thumbnails and avatar** (`brand-profile.md`).
- **Music channels**: the artist or the current release's cover art carries the banner; the genre
  sets the mood (dark and contrasting, neon and gradients, calm and elegant); leave room for a
  release line that can be changed with each release.

### Generating it

Ask `poyo` for a wide 16:9 image (the widest it offers) and describe the scene in plain language:

> A wide panoramic banner for a lo-fi music channel: a dim room at night with a turntable and warm
> lamp light on the right third, deep teal and amber tones, soft focus, a large calm empty area in
> the middle for a title, no text, no logos, no watermark.

- Ask for **no text** in the image when the name must be exact; image models misspell. Put the text
  on afterwards, or ask for it in quotes and check every letter.
- Name the empty area ("large empty area in the middle") so the composition leaves room for text.
- Say what to avoid in words: clutter, too many colours, visible seams, cut-off subjects at the edges.
- Run `banner_preview.py` on every candidate. A candidate whose subject or text falls outside the
  green box is rejected or regenerated, not cropped harder.
- Do not retry a failed or poor generation automatically: report what came out and ask the owner.

## Profile picture

Seen as a circle at 98 px on the channel page and much smaller beside comments and in lists.

- **One simple shape**: a face, a mark or one or two letters. It must still be recognisable in
  `avatar-sizes.png` at 36 px.
- **Nothing in the corners**: the circle cuts them off (`edges_outside_circle` should be near 0);
  keep the subject inside the central ~80 % of the circle.
- **Contrast**: subject against a plain background; check it on both the light and the dark row.
  `contrast_stddev_at_36px` near 0 means it reads as a flat dot.
- **No small text, no taglines** — unreadable at that size.
- **A person**: a tight head-and-shoulders crop, face filling most of the circle, looking at the
  camera, even soft light, a plain or softly blurred background, natural skin. No sunglasses, no
  full-body shots.
- **A logo or mark**: centred, with even margin, on a brand colour. Use `--fit contain --pad-color`
  for a wide logo rather than squeezing it.
- **Consistent** with the banner and the music platform profiles: the same picture or mark across
  YouTube, Spotify and Apple Music helps people know it is the same artist.

Prompt example for `poyo` (square output):

> A square profile picture: a bold geometric fox head in amber on a deep teal background, centred,
> flat vector style, thick clean shapes, generous empty margin around it, no text.
